## Method and limits

- SQL: `SELECT amount, si, title, valid_from, valid_to, provision, caveat, transitional FROM limits WHERE coordinate = ? AND valid_from <= ? AND (valid_to IS NULL OR ? < valid_to)`.
- Passes: one untimed warm-up, then the timed passes with the garbage collector paused. Machine: Darwin arm64 (Apple M4), Python 3.11.15.
- The table holds Orders from SI 2010/2926 (in force 1 February 2011); earlier dates return no row. It was built knowing which sections the battery asks about; it holds every Order from 2011 to 2026 for them, not only the probe dates, and its figures are copied from the Orders' Schedules with the source bytes' SHA-256.
- Each Order applies by its "appropriate date" (for an unfair dismissal award, the effective date of termination), not the date a question is asked; the Order's transitional clause is returned with every row. Choosing that date is the caller's.
- Not tested: colloquial questions with no citation; Control 4 (the tool-call limit belongs to the orchestrator); Northern Ireland limits.
